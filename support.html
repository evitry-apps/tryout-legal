Common questions

A try-on takes a while. About 30 seconds for one piece, 25 more for each extra piece. You can leave the app or lock your iPhone: it keeps going, and the app picks it up when you come back.

A try-on failed. Your credit is given back. Try again, or use a clearer photo of the garment on a plain background.

Swimwear or lingerie is refused. That is on purpose: Tryout does not try them on.

Keep my wardrobe on a new iPhone. Settings > Account > sign in with Apple, Google or Facebook, then sign in the same way on the new iPhone.

Delete my data. Settings > Delete all my data. See delete your data.

Cancel a subscription. In the iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions.
