Privacy Policy

The Tryout app · Last updated: 1 October 2026

In short

  • Your photos stay private. They are only used for your try-ons, and you can delete them at any time.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics tools, nothing sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can use Tryout without an account. Signing in with Apple, Google or Facebook is optional.
  • Settings > Delete all my data erases everything, at once.

Who runs Tryout

Tryout is developed and run by Eloi Vitry, an individual developer, who is responsible for your data. Contact: eloivitry@mac.com.

What we use, and why

Photos of you. The model photo (and side photo) you take or generate in the app is used only to show clothes on you. When you create a model photo, up to 3 of the photos you picked are kept until you choose a version, then deleted. Photos you look through to choose one are checked on the fly and never stored.

Clothes. Photos of clothes you want to try (camera, library, a shared link or product page) and of the pieces you own, with their name, category, colours, how you like to wear them, and the cut-out or improved copies of those photos.

Results. Try-on images, the advice on them, what you save, like or dislike (so the next advice fits your taste), and a cache of try-on steps so the same step is not redone.

Your details. First name (to greet you), last name (only on a share card if you choose to show it), age (18 or over), gender and country of origin (to adapt cuts and style references, never to make assumptions). You can change them in Settings > Your details. Your first and last name are never sent to an AI service.

Sign-in (optional). If you connect Apple, Google or Facebook, we receive from that service an identifier, your email address (with Apple it may be a private relay address) and your name if it provides it. They are used only to recognise you on another iPhone, never to contact you. For Apple, we keep a token so we can revoke the link when you delete your account.

Approximate location (optional). Only when you tap “Suggest an outfit” and allow it, your iPhone sends a position rounded to about 10 km, to get today's weather. It is not stored. If you do not allow it, the city of your iPhone's time zone is used instead.

Usage counters. How many try-ons and outfit checks you used today, for the daily limits. The app language, and the time you confirmed you are 18 or over.

Purchases. If subscriptions are offered, the App Store handles payment; RevenueCat records which purchases belong to your app identifier.

What Tryout does not accept

Tryout is for adults (18+). Swimwear and lingerie cannot be tried on: the app refuses them. Photos must be of you, dressed.

Where it is stored, and for how long

On Supabase servers in the EU, in private storage that only your account can read. Photos reach the app through links valid for 10 minutes. Everything is kept until you delete it: one piece at a time, by replacing your model photo, or all at once.

Delete your data

Settings > Delete all my data deletes all your photos, pieces, results and details, your account and your sign-in links (Sign in with Apple is revoked), immediately. It does not cancel an App Store subscription: do that in your Apple ID settings. More: how to delete your data.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to eloivitry@mac.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL). The legal basis is the contract (providing the app you asked for) and, for the optional location, your consent, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings.

Children

Tryout is not for anyone under 18. The app asks for confirmation and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the app shows the new version through this same link.
