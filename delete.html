Delete your data

Tryout

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Tryout > Settings.
  2. Tap Delete all my data, then Delete everything.

Everything is deleted at once and for good: your photos, model photo, pieces, try-ons, saved items, details, your account and its links to Apple, Google or Facebook (Sign in with Apple is revoked). Nothing is kept in a backup we can restore.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app (for example you signed in with Facebook or Google and removed it), write to eloivitry@mac.com from the email address of that sign-in, asking to delete your Tryout data. It is done within 30 days and we confirm by email.

What is not deleted

An App Store subscription is not cancelled: do it in the iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Daily cost counters with no user identifier are kept.
